Privacy Policy

How information is handled in our internal Windows workspace.

1. About this policy

This Privacy Policy describes how Bovo AI Operations handles information in connection with our own Amazon business operations. The application is operated by 内蒙古魏帛绒毛制品有限公司. It is a Windows desktop analysis workspace for the Amazon Japan marketplace, used internally for our own business. It is not a SaaS service offered to other sellers.

2. Amazon API access and purposes

Current functions include retrieving sales and traffic data through Amazon SP-API, querying FBA inventory, analyzing Search Query Performance (SQP) and Search Catalog Performance (SCP), managing SKU costs, and analyzing product performance. We use these functions to understand sales, inventory, search visibility, costs, and business performance. We plan to use Amazon Ads API to read performance data for our own advertising accounts, subject to the required access and account authorization. This policy does not represent that Amazon Ads API access has been approved, or that Amazon sponsors or endorses this application.

3. Categories of information

The application processes business information such as sales and traffic metrics, advertising reports imported by the operator, inventory quantities, SQP and SCP search-performance metrics and search terms, product identifiers and attributes, SKU costs, fee records, and product mappings. Depending on the function, it also stores uploaded source files, import previews and validation records, account configuration, synchronization records, saved AI diagnostic inputs and results, exports, and database backups. Planned Amazon Ads API access may add advertising campaign and targeting identifiers and performance metrics, subject to authorization.

The application is intended for our own business analysis, not for collecting buyer identity or contact details. We do not intentionally import buyer names, delivery addresses, telephone numbers, or email addresses. Current supported business templates do not have dedicated fields for these buyer details, and the current Amazon API connector does not request buyer-information or shipping-address endpoints.

However, manual CSV and XLSX uploads are retained as complete local source files before preview validation. Preview records can store the first ten data rows across all parsed columns, including columns not mapped to business metrics. Text fields and uploaded files are not subject to a general personal-information detection or redaction mechanism. If an uploaded file or text field contains personal information, it may therefore remain in local source files, previews, stored records, exports, or backups even if it is not a supported business field. Users should exclude unnecessary personal information before importing files; the application does not guarantee automatic removal of such information.

4. Local storage and safeguards

Business records and AI diagnostic history are stored in a local SQLite database on the Windows computer. Source reports, certain inventory snapshots, exports, and backups may also be stored as local files. Amazon credentials and AI API keys are protected using Windows Data Protection API (DPAPI) in the current Windows user context. This credential protection does not mean the entire business database, source reports, or backups are encrypted by the application.

The local backend checks permitted hosts and request origins and uses a local session token for protected requests. These controls do not replace the Windows account and device access controls. Protection of local files also depends on the operator's Windows permissions, device configuration, and handling of copies and backups. No claim is made that full-disk encryption, cloud-backup restrictions, or other device-level settings have been enabled.

Local storage does not mean that all processing remains on the computer. Amazon API communications and confirmed AI requests use external services over HTTPS; communication between the application and its local backend uses a loopback connection. This policy makes no claim of security certification, independent audit, or absolute protection.

5. Account authorization and access control

Amazon account access is intended only for our own business and is subject to the permissions authorized for the relevant integration. The operator is responsible for limiting access to the Windows device and application to persons authorized to use these business records and account connections. Credentials are used by the backend to communicate with the relevant provider and are not published on this website.

Stopping local synchronization, revoking provider-side account authorization, and deleting previously stored local records are separate actions. Provider-side authorization must be managed through the applicable provider's account controls. Stopping synchronization or revoking authorization does not itself delete existing local records, exports, or backups. AI diagnostic requests require explicit confirmation in the application before they are sent.

6. Retention and deletion

Local business data, saved reports, AI diagnostic history, and database backups remain stored unless actively deleted. The application does not apply a single automatic expiration or deletion period to these records. This describes normal retention behavior, not a guarantee of permanent preservation or recovery.

Temporary caches, temporary download files, and other transient files may be cleared according to the mechanisms actually implemented by the application or operating environment. Not every temporary file is necessarily cleared automatically, and this policy does not promise that every file will be kept indefinitely.

The operator may manage and delete local data using methods actually available for the relevant data or files, including appropriate local file administration. The current application does not provide a general-purpose control for deleting all stored business data or AI diagnostic history. Cancelling an AI diagnostic or allowing its confirmation preview to expire does not delete the saved record. Copies in saved reports, exports, and backups are separate and may remain until separately removed. Local data management should take account of active services and database consistency; this policy does not promise secure erasure or automatic deletion of all copies.

Deleting local information or revoking an account authorization does not by itself delete information already received by Amazon, DeepSeek, or another configured service provider. DeepSeek's retention and handling of information it receives are governed by its own applicable privacy policy and service terms. If an intermediary is used, its applicable policies also govern its handling of the information. This application does not control those providers' retention or deletion processes. Privacy questions and requests concerning records managed by the operator may be sent to the contact in Section 10.

7. User-initiated AI analysis

The application supports user-initiated business diagnostics using DeepSeek AI through the configured service endpoint. After the user selects an analysis scope and confirms the request, the backend transmits a prepared business-data snapshot or a selected evidence packet, together with analysis instructions and model parameters. Depending on the selected sales or product analysis, this can include date ranges, sales and traffic metrics, data-quality indicators, and product identifiers or attributes such as SKU, ASIN, color, and style. The reviewed request flow does not attach the entire database, the uploaded source workbook, or prior diagnostic conversations. A connection test uses a test prompt without business data.

Selected text fields can still carry information entered by the operator; these requests should not be treated as guaranteed anonymized data. The application saves diagnostic snapshots and results locally. An AI provider also receives the submitted request, so information sent for analysis is not stored exclusively on the local computer. Processing may involve another country or region depending on the configured service.

DeepSeek's handling and retention of information it receives are subject to its applicable privacy policy and service terms. If the configured endpoint uses an intermediary, that intermediary also receives the request and its applicable policies govern its handling. Deleting local records does not delete copies held by a provider. This policy does not promise zero retention or exclusion from model training; the applicable provider terms and settings must be checked for the service in use.

8. Disclosure and sale

We do not sell user data as an advertising data product. Data is used for our own business analysis and the integrations described in this policy. Amazon receives the requests necessary for its authorized API services, and the configured AI service receives the information submitted when the user initiates AI analysis. Information may also be disclosed where required by applicable law. This policy does not claim that no information is ever transmitted to third parties.

9. This public website

This website contains only a software introduction and privacy information. It does not provide access to the desktop application or its business database. These pages contain no analytics scripts, advertising trackers, forms, external fonts, or application login. When published on GitHub Pages, the hosting provider may process technical information about visits under its own privacy statement. Application credentials, seller account identifiers, business records, and private addresses are not published on this website.
